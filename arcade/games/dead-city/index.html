Dead City

Manhattan turned in one night. You start on the street as a regular guy with an M4 and a pistol. When you die, something wakes up in you: pick a power and keep fighting. They run, they climb over each other, and they pile on anyone they can reach.

Start as the Survivor and you wake up with one of the three powers when he dies. Picking a power here skips the human part.
