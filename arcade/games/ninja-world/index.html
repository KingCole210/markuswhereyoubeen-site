NINJA WORLD

The clan wars are over and the Leaf has just been founded. Every clan has its district in the new village. Take D, C and B-rank missions from the mission desk, train, craft, and hunt the bandits who prey on the roads and the missing-nin who lead them.
Each player: press A on a controller, or Enter on the keyboard, to join, then make your ninja: clan, path (regular or protégé), outfit and four Genin jutsu. 1v1 TEST drops you into a duel to try any jutsu.
