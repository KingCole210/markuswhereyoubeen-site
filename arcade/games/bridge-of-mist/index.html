THE HUNTERS’ LODGE

The bounty board
The host picks the bounty and sets out.
You’re a squad of hunter ninja: you track down rogue ninja, and maybe one day a tailed beast. It's hard on purpose. Every swing, dodge and block costs stamina. Block just as the white glint flashes to parry: they stagger and your next hit is a riposte. Enemies hit hard and gold-ringed elites harder. A downed friend has 12 s to be revived, or they're out until the next gate. Pills are few: shrines refill them, and if everyone falls you start again at the last shrine.
