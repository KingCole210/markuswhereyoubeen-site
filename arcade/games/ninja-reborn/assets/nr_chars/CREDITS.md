# Ninja Reborn character assets

## The ninja (since 2026-10-21: "I want them all to look like the naruto characters. Everybody should look like that")

Every ninja is made from parts of the Free Fire Naruto models on Sketchfab, all licensed CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

**What was changed:** each model was cut into parts (face, hair, top, bottom, shoes and so on), refitted to one shared skeleton per sex, and re-skinned. Madara and Minato were also given skin weights. Some male parts were fitted to the female skeleton too. The geometry was quantized, and the original textures were kept.

The game recolours the parts for each original character, and paints the eyes (including the Sharingan and Byakugan) over the drawn ones. The builder is `Design/tools/ff_kit/`.

## Other assets

- `kunai.json`: **"Exposive Kunai"** by matoteus (https://sketchfab.com/matoteus), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/exposive-kunai-819165867a3041eeb984a42afa65055c, licensed CC BY 4.0.
  - **Changes:** the textures were downsized to 512px and the model was repacked as glTF .json.
  - The game lays it along the throw.
- `headband_plates.json`: the forehead protector plates from the user's `naruto-headbands.zip` (`HEADBANDTIME2.stl`).
  - The download did not record a source or licence.
  - **Changes:** split into one plate per village and simplified (85%).
  - Added at the user's request (2026-10-21). The original ninja wear their village's plate on the band the game cuts from their forehead.

## Retired

The realistic bodies used briefly on 2026-10-21 are no longer used. These were Quaternius' Universal Base Characters (CC0) and Marc Ed's "Akatsuki coat - Itachi's clothes" (CC BY 4.0). Their files were removed. Itachi's own cloak from the Free Fire set is the Akatsuki coat now.
